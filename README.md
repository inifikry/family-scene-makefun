# Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2`.

## What it does

`main.py` writes one short family scene (two lines, 173 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Dad fell asleep halfway through the bedtime story again, so the kids tucked the blanket around him
> and whispered the ending themselves: "and the dragon said goodnight, Dad."

A small domestic moment: Dad keeps falling asleep during the bedtime
story, so for once the kids are the ones doing the tucking in — and they
give him the ending he missed. Warm, a little funny, and specific enough
to feel like a real family, not a greeting card.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The output is fully deterministic: repeated runs produce byte-identical
`/output/entry.txt`.
